🚀 Expo EAS Setup

To publish your app to the App Store, you need an Expo account with EAS (Expo Application Services).

Get your token from expo.dev/settings/access-tokens
Lowercase, no spaces. Used in your app's URL on Expo.

Don't have an Expo account?

  1. Go to expo.dev/signup
  2. Create a free account
  3. Go to Settings → Access Tokens
  4. Create a new token and paste it above
